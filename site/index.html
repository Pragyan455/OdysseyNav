I  ·  THE SEA

Krakens rise on a clock.

You steer the galley yourself. Krakens surface and sink on a schedule, so timing matters as much as the route: wait for the passage to open, or risk the hull and your crew.

Food runs down every second at sea. Rough water cracks the hull. Wood and rope keep you afloat.

II  ·  THE GODS

Pray to Zeus. Chronos takes the helm.

Earn the gods' favor and you can pray. Lightning strikes the mast and Chronos, an AI navigator, plots a golden course and sails it for you.

Lose their favor and Poseidon's cyclone starts hunting you. If it catches you, you find your way home by the stars.

III  ·  THE ISLANDS

Eight islands. Every choice costs something.

On each leg two islands rise from the mist. Read the omen, pick one, and land. Each is an episode of the Odyssey (the Cyclops, Circe, the Sirens) with a choice that trades food, crew, favor and supplies.

Keep food, health, crew and favor above zero all the way to Ithaca.

Under the hood

An AI that plans through time.

Most pathfinding plans around obstacles in space. Chronos plans in space and time: it knows when each kraken will rise, so it can wait for one to sink instead of taking the long way round.

CHRONOS

Time-aware A*

A search over position and time, with three temperaments to choose from: survival, balanced and speed.

MADE IN CODE

No downloaded assets

Every ship, island and monster is built from geometry in Panda3D. The title art is painted with Pillow, and the music and sound effects are synthesized with NumPy.

RULES

Tested, not guessed

The voyage rules live apart from the graphics in plain Python, with 92 automated tests behind them.

Play it yourself

Set sail from your own machine.

It needs Python 3. These commands are for macOS and Linux; on Windows, use .venv\Scripts\python in place of .venv/bin/python.

# get the game
git clone -b 3d-on-teammate-m3 https://github.com/Pragyan455/OdysseyNav
cd OdysseyNav

# install and run
python3 -m venv .venv
.venv/bin/python -m pip install -r requirements.txt
.venv/bin/python main3d.py
